03 ARCHITECTURE

Enterprise discipline
from day one.

Architecture is defined around approved operating boundaries. It is not an assertion that every planned business workflow has been built.

01

Organization-aware isolation

Explicit tenancy and membership boundaries.

02

Capability-based authorization

Governed responsibilities instead of unrestricted roles.

03

Version-controlled product definition

Technical revisions are managed separately from manufacturing release.

04

Planning and execution separation

Allocation, manufacturing orders and shop-floor events have distinct authority.

05

Evidence and auditability

Critical changes are designed to be traceable and reviewable.
